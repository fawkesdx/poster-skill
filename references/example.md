# Example shape

This is a shape for the brief and the spec. It is not a topic, and it is not wording to reuse. If the user's source does not say it, it does not go on their poster.

## Brief

**Sheet:** A0 portrait, 841 × 1189 mm. Assumed, because no venue size was given.

**Reader:** someone in the field who stops for a minute.

**Source:** a paper.

**Takeaway:** On the same measurements, procedure B separates two groups that procedure A leaves mixed.

**Key ideas:**

1. Both procedures use the same measurements.
2. Procedure A produces one mixed result.
3. Procedure B produces two groups.
4. The two results are shown side by side, at the same scale.
5. The source reports no score, so the poster reports no score.

**Must not imply:**

- A mechanism the source does not show.
- That B wins on every dataset.
- Any number that is not in the source.

**Off the sheet:** parameters, the other figures, the bibliography.

## Two layouts

1. **Gallery.** One band per paper figure. Rejected: the claim shrinks, and extra figures do not change it.
2. **Bands (kept).** Top: a four-box flow of the shared measurement. Bottom: the two results, equal width, same scale. The takeaway is the bottom pair.
